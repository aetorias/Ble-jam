╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                  CAPACITOR PLACEMENT (CRITICAL!)                          ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Place capacitors as close as possible to nRF24 modules:

nRF24 #1:  100µF cap between VCC and GND (+ near VCC pin)
           0.1µF cap between VCC and GND (for high-freq stability)

nRF24 #2:  100µF cap between VCC and GND (+ near VCC pin)
           0.1µF cap between VCC and GND (for high-freq stability)

⚠️  Without proper capacitors, modules will randomly reset or fail!
